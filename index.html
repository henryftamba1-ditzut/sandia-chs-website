ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Education, Skills and Character

Sandia Community High School is committed to providing meaningful educational opportunities that combine academic learning, practical skills and character development.

WHO WE ARE

Sandia Community High School

Sandia Community High School is a community- focused secondary school located in Foya Statutory District, Lofa County, Liberia.

The school provides academic education while expanding opportunities for learners to acquire practical technical and vocational skills. Through this approach, students are encouraged to develop knowledge, competencies, creativity discipline and responsible citizenship.

Our educational approach recognizes that successful learners need both academic knowledge and practical competencies that can prepare them for further education, employment, entrepreneurship and community development.

OUR MISSION

Developing Capable Learners

To provide quality academic and practical education that develops knowledgeable, skilled, disciplined and responsible learners who can contribute positively to society.

OUR VISION

A Future-Ready Generation

To become a learning institution that equips young people with the knowledge, practical competencies, character and confidence needed to succeed in a changing world.

OUR CORE VALUES

Principles That Guide Us

Excellence

Integrity

We promote honesty, accountability and responsible conduct.

Innovation

We encourage creativity, technology and practical approaches to solving problems.

Discipline

Service
