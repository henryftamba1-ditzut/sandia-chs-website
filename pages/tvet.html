OUR TVET PROGRAM

Learning by Doing

Sandia Community High School recognizes the importance of technical and vocational education in preparing young people for employment, entrepreneurship and productive participation in their communities.

Our TVET approach emphasizes practical learning, technical knowledge, problem solving, creativity, safety and responsible use of tools and equipment.

Learners are encouraged to move beyond theoretical understanding by practicing skills and producing practical work that demonstrates competence.

Practical Skills Developing hands-on technical competencies through practical activities.
Employability Preparing learners with skills relevant to employment and the modern workplace.
Entrepreneurship Encouraging creativity, innovation and opportunities for self-employment.

Learners can develop practical skills across fourteen technical, construction, service, digital, agricultural and creative vocational areas.

Learners can develop practical skills across a range of technical, construction, service and creative vocational areas.

COMPETENCY-BASED TRAINING

From Knowledge to Demonstrated Skill

TVET learning should provide learners with repeated opportunities to understand, practice and demonstrate technical competencies.

01

Learn

Understand technical concepts, tools, materials, procedures and safety requirements.

02

Practice

Perform guided practical activities and develop confidence through repeated practice.

03

Demonstrate

Complete practical tasks and demonstrate the required competencies to an appropriate standard.

04

Create

Apply technical skills to projects, products, services and potential entrepreneurial activities.

FUTURE READINESS

Skills for Employment and Entrepreneurship

TVET education can help learners develop practical competencies that are useful in employment, apprenticeship, further technical education and entrepreneurship.

In addition to technical skills, learners should develop workplace habits such as responsibility, teamwork, communication, problem solving, creativity and respect for safety procedures.

These complementary competencies strengthen the learner's ability to apply technical knowledge effectively in real-world environments.

T

Technical Skills

Practical knowledge and competence in selected occupational areas.

W

Work Readiness

Professional behavior, teamwork, communication and responsibility.

E

Enterprise

Creativity, innovation and skills that can support self-employment.
