WHO WE ARE

Education for Knowledge, Skills and Service

Sandia Community High School is an educational institution serving learners within the Foya Statutory District of Lofa County, Liberia.

The school combines academic education with technical and vocational education and training to provide learners with opportunities to develop knowledge, practical competencies, creativity, discipline and responsible citizenship.

Through a balanced approach to education, Sandia Community High School seeks to prepare learners for further education, productive work, entrepreneurship and meaningful participation in community development.

Academic Education Knowledge, critical thinking and preparation for further learning.
Technical Education Practical competencies for work, innovation and entrepreneurship.
Community Service Developing responsible learners prepared to contribute to their communities.
