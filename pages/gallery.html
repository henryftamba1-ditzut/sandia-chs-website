OUR VISUAL STORY

Learning, Skills and School Life

Photographs can provide a powerful record of learning, practical activities, achievements and important moments within a school community.

The Sandia Community High School gallery will provide an organized space for presenting authentic images from the school's academic, technical-vocational and community activities.

Only appropriate and authorized photographs should be published on the official school website.

Authentic Use genuine photographs representing actual school activities and facilities.
Organized Group photographs according to meaningful school activities and categories.
Responsible Publish photographs appropriately while respecting student privacy and dignity.

DIGITAL MEDIA MANAGEMENT

Building an Organized School Media Library

As the website grows, photographs can be organized according to activity, date, category and other relevant information.

This approach will make it easier for school administrators to maintain an accurate and useful visual record of school activities.

In the future, the gallery can be connected to the school's backend system so authorized administrators can upload, categorize, update and remove photographs through a secure dashboard.
