OUR ACADEMIC PROGRAM

Education That Builds Strong Foundations

Sandia Community High School provides academic education designed to develop learners' knowledge, understanding, communication skills and capacity for independent and critical thinking.

Academic learning is an important foundation for further education, professional development, responsible citizenship and lifelong learning.

Classroom learning is complemented by practical activities and opportunities that encourage learners to connect knowledge with real-world situations.

Knowledge Building a strong understanding of academic concepts and subject content.
Critical Thinking Encouraging learners to analyze, question, reason and solve problems.
Communication Developing effective written, oral and collaborative communication skills.

LEARNER DEVELOPMENT

Key Academic Competencies

Our academic approach aims to develop learners beyond memorization by strengthening transferable knowledge and skills.

01

Problem Solving

Learners develop the ability to identify problems, analyze information and apply appropriate solutions.

02

Communication

Learners strengthen their ability to express ideas clearly through written, oral and collaborative activities.

03

Digital Literacy

Learners are encouraged to develop appropriate digital skills for learning, communication and modern work environments.

04

Independent Learning

Learners develop study habits, research skills and personal responsibility for continuous learning.

LEARNING APPROACH

Connecting Classroom Learning with Practice

Student-Centered Learning

Effective education should give learners opportunities to participate, investigate, practice, discuss and demonstrate what they have learned.

The academic program therefore provides a foundation that can be strengthened through practical activities, projects, collaborative learning and appropriate use of technology.

L

Learning

Understanding concepts and developing meaningful knowledge.

P

Practice

Applying knowledge through activities and practical exercises.

D

Demonstration

Demonstrating competence through assignments, projects and assessments.
